# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct sets out what is expected of everyone who works on Vaneta OS: members, contractors, and anyone taking part in its discussions and reviews.

## Our commitment

People will install Vaneta OS on the computers they rely on every day. The way we work with each other should be as careful as the way we treat their systems. We expect everyone to:

- Act with integrity and own their mistakes, in code and in conversation.
- Treat others with courtesy, whatever their role, experience or background.
- Share what they know, and give feedback that helps the work get better.
- Use language that does not exclude or demean anyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful in discussions, reviews and every other channel.
- Review the change, not the person who wrote it.
- Report bugs through issues and security problems privately ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).
- Keep confidential information, user data and credentials confidential.
- Follow the contribution rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats or intimidation of any kind.
- Discrimination based on race, ethnicity, gender, religion, sexual orientation, age or disability.
- Sharing confidential information, user data or credentials outside the project, including in screenshots or chat.
- Using real user data or accounts for testing without authorization.
- Spamming, trolling, or disrupting discussions.
- Bypassing review, CI, or branch rules.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation:

1. Report it to the project lead privately, as soon as you can.
2. Include what you can: date and time, who was involved, what happened, and screenshots or logs with personal data removed.
3. Do not try to settle it in public.

Reports are kept confidential and looked into promptly.

## Enforcement

A violation may lead to removal of access to repositories and services, a temporary suspension of contribution rights, or further action as the project lead decides.

By contributing to Vaneta OS, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.
